Mode: Persuade (light). Audience: the friend group on phones from a group-chat link. ## Direction contract

THESIS: Turkey Links is the circular's Seasonal Aisle page: a self-improvement sale for a turkey who keeps losing to his friends. It refuses the plain link list and the card grid of icons.

OWN-WORLD: Same Uncle Turkey Market inks: ink date strip, a red doorbuster box billed 3x (the Stop Losing To Your Friends Kit), a yellow starburst price, newsprint price boxes with a drawn SVG product cut, a red price, an aisle tag on the top border, a recalled ink box for the Brandon book, dashed rain-check slips, and an ink "Imported Goods" band of real links.

STORY: The visitor reads the doorbuster kit and its fine print, scans the specials (ping pong book, grudge lure, programmer book), presses Add to cart, gets a rain check, then follows a real turkey link.

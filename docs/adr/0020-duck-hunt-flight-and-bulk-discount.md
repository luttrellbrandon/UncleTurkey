# Birds rise and escape like a duck hunt; streaks earn a Bulk Discount

Turkey Hunt should feel like a classic light-gun duck hunt, not a side-scrolling gallery. We considered keeping horizontal fly-throughs, adding a dog-style sidekick, and having birds rise out of the scenery, bank off the walls, and escape upward when their time runs out. We chose rise-and-escape. A bird that escapes is still a **Fly-off**, and the Shot, Point Quota and retry rules are unchanged (ADRs 0016–0018).

We also add one small reward. Every three positive birds bagged in a row, without a wasted Shot in between, earns a **Bulk Discount** of +50. Hitting Brandon Turkey, wasting a Shot near a bird, or letting a bird fly off resets the streak. The bonus is small enough that the Point Quotas stay meaningful.

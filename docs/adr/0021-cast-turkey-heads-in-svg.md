# The cast are drawn as turkey heads in SVG, not faces or photos

The site needs each friend to be recognizable on Turkey Hunt birds, comic turkeys and the landing page. We considered shipping the real head-shot photos, putting illustrated human faces on turkey bodies, and drawing every cast member as a turkey with their signature traits. We chose turkey heads: Daniel's brown quiff and rectangular glasses, Brandon's dark spiky crest and big grin, Steph's swept-back ponytail, sunglasses and gold hoop, and Steve's shiny bald head and gray stubble. Each head is hand-authored SVG in `src/lib/assets/characters/faces.ts`, so it reads as a turkey first and a friend second. This keeps ADRs 0009 and 0013: Source Likeness photos stay in the gitignored `.private/likeness/` folder and never ship.
